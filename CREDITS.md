## The elevation profile

The cross-section drawn in the hero, the closing band and the transect is
**schematic**. It is generated by `tools/build-graphics.mjs` from the real
elevation range this constituency occupies on the west flank of Mount Elgon
and from the concave form of a shield volcano's flank. It is not survey data,
it is not derived from a digital elevation model, and it is captioned as
schematic everywhere it appears.

The elevation bands used to place register entries, pillars and projects on
the slope are indicative, read from published topographic ranges for the
flank. The constituency office has not confirmed them.
