Editorial standard

Every update should answer five questions.

1
What happened?A short factual headline and summary.
2
Where?Sub-county, parish or national institution.
3
Why does it matter?Connect it to a development pillar.
4
What happens next?A specific follow-up or milestone.
5
What is the evidence?Photos, letters, Parliament links or verified status data.
