Budadiri County East · Sironko District West flank, Mount Elgon

We farm the shoulder of the mountain.

Budadiri East runs from the valley road up to the edge of the forest. Nearly everything this office deals with — the price paid for a bag of coffee, a culvert that has gone, a clinic two hours away on foot — is decided by where on that slope you are standing. So that is how this site is arranged: by altitude, from the boundary down to the road.

How this site is arranged

Four bands, from the boundary down to the road.

Every project, risk and register entry here is placed in one of them. It is the quickest way to tell whether a promise makes sense: a water scheme at 1,900 metres is a different proposition from the same scheme at 1,200, and a road that is passable in January is not the same road in April.

The bands are read from published topographic ranges for the Elgon west flank and are indicative. They place a matter on the slope so it can be argued about sensibly — they are not survey figures, and the constituency office has not yet confirmed them.
